# SourceLoop Demo Script — 2:30–2:45

This script matches the recorded live-application demo. The visual shows SourceLoop; no static mockup is used.

## Opening — 0:00–0:15

**Visual:** SourceLoop desktop workspace with the first lesson open.

> Students don’t just need another explanation. When they get a question wrong, they need the exact sentence they missed. SourceLoop makes that return path part of the study loop.

## Read and verify — 0:15–0:35

**Visual:** Click the source anchor and expand the exact passage.

> Here, the lesson says that a weighted graph is a set of places and costs. One click opens the exact source. The claim and its evidence stay connected.

## Compute, don’t animate — 0:35–1:10

**Visual:** Switch to the frontier lesson and step the Dijkstra visual to the end.

> The shortest-path visual is computed by the local server. Each step exposes settled nodes, tentative distances, and edge relaxations. The final route is Library to Canteen to Lab, with total cost nine. We didn’t record a video of the answer; we exposed the computation.

## Practice and review — 1:10–1:45

**Visual:** Submit a wrong answer, inspect source-backed feedback, then retry correctly.

> Practice is graded on the server. Answers and reasoning appear only after submission. A wrong choice creates one source-linked review item. Retrying correctly resolves it, so the learner still has a clean path forward.

## Bring your own notes — 1:45–2:10

**Visual:** Open import, select the Markdown fixture, and submit the imported pack.

> Students can bring their own notes. SourceLoop creates literal quote and source-location checks instead of inventing a new explanation. The notes remain the source of truth.

## Study offline — 2:10–2:32

**Visual:** Export the pack and open the downloaded file in the offline browser view.

> The whole pack exports as one HTML file. It still runs the graph, switches languages, and grades practice without a server. That makes the loop portable.

## Close — 2:32–2:45

**Visual:** Return to the workspace and show the review queue and export control.

> SourceLoop: turn your notes into a cited, interactive study loop.
